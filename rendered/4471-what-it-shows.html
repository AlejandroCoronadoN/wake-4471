The three problems

parser · dates parse month first for day-first tenants. The shared parser reads every date as mm/dd. All eleven tenants on file write the day first (dayFirst asks the platform, per locale), so 07/04 becomes 4 July. Every channel, every tenant, April to October. One code fix in the parser.

hint · booking-v3 prints the wrong hint under the date field. Through booking-v3 the form tells people to type MM/DD/YYYY; they do, and the form reads it the tenant's way, day first. Only the three tenants on booking-v3 (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour), only the form channel. Fixing the parser does not fix this: the hint is wrong, not the parse.

one-day-move · the day moves by one, the month does not. Five reports describe a shift of one day with the month intact, or a time-zone split: "Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May", "Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday", "The invite arrived before I made the booking", "Every booking in June is one day off". Not a parse order problem. A date is being treated as an instant and crossing midnight somewhere. Under-reported: the last one says "I have stopped reporting these".
